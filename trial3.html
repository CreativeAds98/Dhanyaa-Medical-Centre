EECP Therapy

A non-surgical way to a stronger heart.

Enhanced External Counterpulsation — a non-invasive, outpatient treatment for chronic stable angina and coronary artery disease, especially for patients not suited to angioplasty or bypass surgery.

  • Improves blood flow and builds natural collateral circulation
  • Reduces frequency and severity of angina
  • Improves exercise tolerance and quality of life
