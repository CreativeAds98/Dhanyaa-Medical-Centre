Deep dive

Every diagnostic test, explained clearly.

What each test is, what it detects, how long it takes, and how to prepare — no guesswork before your visit.

HomeDiagnostics
What it is

A simple, painless test that records the heart's electrical signals using small electrodes on your chest, arms and legs.

What it detects

Irregular heart rhythms, signs of a past or ongoing heart attack, heart enlargement.

Duration

5–10 minutes.

Preparation

No special preparation. Avoid oil or lotion on the chest area.

What it is

An ultrasound-based imaging test creating real-time images of the heart's chambers, valves and major vessels.

What it detects

Valve disorders, heart failure, congenital defects, blood clots, pumping efficiency.

Duration

30–45 minutes.

Preparation

No fasting required; wear comfortable clothing.

What it is

Monitors heart performance while you walk on a treadmill at increasing difficulty, simulating exertion.

What it detects

Coronary artery blockages, exercise-induced arrhythmias, cardiovascular fitness.

Duration

30–45 minutes including recovery monitoring.

Preparation

Avoid heavy meals, caffeine and smoking 2–3 hours prior.

What it is

Inflatable cuffs on the calves, thighs and lower body inflate and deflate in sync with your heartbeat, improving blood flow and building collateral circulation.

What it detects

Chronic stable angina and coronary artery disease not suited to surgery.

Duration

35 one-hour sessions over ~7 weeks, 5 sessions/week.

Preparation

Comfortable, loose clothing recommended.

What it is

In-house lab testing across blood sugar, HbA1c, lipid profile, kidney & liver function, thyroid, and routine panels.

What it detects

Diabetic control, cardiovascular risk factors, organ function.

Duration

Most reports same day.

Preparation

Fasting required for some panels — front desk will advise.

What it is

A small, portable device worn on the body that continuously records your heart's electrical activity over an extended period at home.

What it detects

Intermittent irregular heartbeats, palpitations and rhythm disturbances that a routine ECG can miss.

Duration

24–48 hours of continuous wear.

Preparation

No special preparation; continue normal daily activities while wearing the device.

What it is

Specialised wound care and dressing for diabetic foot ulcers, with regular monitoring to support healing.

What it detects

Early signs of infection, poor circulation, and delayed healing risk in the affected foot.

Duration

Ongoing, based on ulcer severity and healing progress.

Preparation

None required; bring previous dressing or wound-care records if available.
